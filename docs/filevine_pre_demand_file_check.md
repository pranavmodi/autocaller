# Pre-Demand File Check for Filevine Case Managers

Use this as a final ten-minute review before the file moves to the demand attorney.

## 1. Treatment and Medical Story

- [ ] Last date of service is current and documented.
- [ ] Every material treatment gap is identified and explained in the file.
- [ ] Referrals, imaging, procedures, discharge, MMI, and future-care recommendations are documented.
- [ ] The treatment chronology supports the claimed injuries and causation.

## 2. Records, Bills, and Balances

- [ ] Records and bills are present for every known provider and every relevant date range.
- [ ] Outstanding, incomplete, duplicate, or unreadable records are flagged with an owner and follow-up date.
- [ ] Bills, ledgers, EOBs, write-offs, and patient balances reconcile to the specials total.
- [ ] Health-plan, Medicare/Medicaid, ERISA, workers' compensation, and other lien issues are identified.

## 3. Liability, Coverage, and Damages

- [ ] Police report, photos, video, witness information, and liability evidence are attached and easy to find.
- [ ] Policy limits, coverage questions, prior claims, and comparative-fault issues are summarized.
- [ ] Wage loss, property damage, out-of-pocket costs, and other claimed damages have supporting documents.
- [ ] Any fact the carrier may use to discount the claim is surfaced rather than buried.

## 4. Attorney Handoff

- [ ] The current case summary states what happened, the injuries, treatment, specials, and demand posture.
- [ ] Every missing item has a named owner, next action, and due date in Filevine.
- [ ] The file answers: What is missing? Why did treatment pause? What remains unresolved? What will the carrier challenge?
- [ ] The attorney can understand the file and its next decision without reconstructing the history from notes.

## Final Ready Check

Before marking the file ready, confirm that the medical story, liability story, and damages total agree with one another. If they do not, keep the file in preparation and document the blocker.
