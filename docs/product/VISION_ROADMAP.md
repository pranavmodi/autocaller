# Possible Minds / Possible OS — Vision and Actionable Roadmap

*2026-06-11, from Pranav's vision statement. This is the orienting document:
read it before reprioritizing anything.*

## The Grand Vision (verbatim intent)

**The Engine** — a cybernetic GTM loop:
listen → understand the market and mindset → sharpen ICP → find top fresh
leads (Front + local DB) → compose and send outreach → observe feedback →
improve the pipeline.

**In parallel:**
- **Products** (market-evidence-driven): PI competitive analysis tool, inbound
  after-hours phone intake system, medical-records chasing system, …
- **Audience**: blog posts / content marketing for organic, SEO on
  getpossibleminds.com, LinkedIn connection-building with leads and thought
  leaders.
- **The Org**: master agent + delegated sub-agents (coding, marketing, GTM,
  SEO) that use and build tools for themselves — a self-learning system.

North star: **maximize the pipeline of PI people to talk to.**
Weekly KPI: qualified engagements/week (learn on), booked conversations
(steer by).

## The anti-overwhelm frame

Three observations that shrink this list:

1. **The Engine is ~80% built.** Listening live (brief, chatbot, alerts);
   ICP tiering exists; Front-warm selection proved itself today; compose →
   edit → schedule → policy-gated send shipped today; auto-observations in
   flight. What remains is closing the improve step and upgrading selection.
2. **Everything else is a multiplier on the Engine, not a parallel
   obligation.** Products come out of what the corpus + conversations prove;
   content comes out of corpus insights; sub-agents come out of automating
   what we already do manually. Nothing starts from scratch.
3. **The org design is already running in miniature**: Claude as
   orchestrator + codex as coding agent + composer/judge/listener as
   specialist tools, with human approval gates. The "master agent + sub-agent
   system" is the formalization of this exact working pattern, not a new
   invention.

The human's job is taste, approvals, and conversations (~5–7 h/week).
The agents' job is everything else. Parallelize agents, never human attention.

## The Plan

### Track A — The Engine (priority 1, this month)
- [in flight] Auto-observations (packet 12) → weekly KPI readable via CLI
- Front lead engine v1: PII scrub gate FIRST, then contact/engagement
  extraction, warm scoring into contact_selection, firm-side ops content into
  listening as primary items (todo to create)
- ICP as versioned artifact (icp_profile vN): seeded from GTM doc + tiers,
  updated monthly from engagement evidence (who replies = truth)
- Weekly improvement ritual (initially manual, 30 min Friday): observations
  summary + brief delta → one policy/skill change → tagged on next week's
  sends. Becomes the master agent's job later.
- Volume ramp 5→15→25/day only after observations show clean deliverability.
- Cadence: daily sends happen by scheduler; Pranav approves batches (15 min/day).

### Track B — Products (wedge bets, gated by evidence)
Rule: a product gets built when (a) the corpus ranks its pain top-3 AND (b) at
least 2 real conversations confirm willingness to engage. Current standings:
- **After-hours intake voice**: corpus-validated + 80% of tech exists
  (autocaller inbound flip). Closest to buildable. Trigger: first reply/consult
  from this week's intake-angle emails.
- **Records chasing system**: top corpus cluster ("center of gravity") +
  Kohanbash call evidence. Needs 1–2 discovery conversations to scope the MVP.
- **PI competitive analysis tool**: no corpus evidence yet — parked until the
  listening system surfaces demand.
Each product = same packet-delegation pattern; Pranav scopes, agents build.

### Track C — Audience (compounding layer, small weekly cadence)
- 1 blog post/week generated from the corpus (top cluster + verbatim quotes →
  insight-led post), human-edited, published on getpossibleminds.com. The
  listening system makes this cheap: the content IS the research.
- SEO: one technical-SEO packet on getpossibleminds.com (audit exists in MC
  seo tables), then monthly checks. Not a daily concern.
- LinkedIn: NOT automatable (ToS + authenticity). Reframe as 30 min/week of
  Pranav's human time, fed by the system: weekly "engage list" (3 thought-leader
  posts to comment on, from listening alerts; 5 warm leads to connect with,
  from Front engagement). The system prepares; the human connects.

### Track D — The Org (master agent + sub-agents, grows out of A)
- Phase 1 (this month): finish master-agent wake-context slices (existing WIP);
  give it the lead-gen goal = Track A's daily loop as its heartbeat, approval-
  gated. It runs what we ran manually today.
- Phase 2: listening goal (5.4) — alerts/brief deltas → proposals.
- Phase 3 (quarter): named sub-agents as *delegation targets with budgets*
  (coding=codex packets, GTM=lead-gen loop, marketing=Track C generation,
  SEO=audit runner) — formalizing the orchestrator pattern proven this week.
  Self-built tools arrive here via the packet system (agents author packets;
  human approves; codex builds).
