# Decisions — 2026-06-30

## D-2026-06-30-01 — Non-leadership lead-gen emails become gatekeeper-style founder-email asks
- **Area:** lead-gen
- **Status:** accepted
- **Decision:** When the selected recipient is a non-leadership staff persona (records, intake, case manager, lien/settlement, paralegal, marketing, or non-partner attorney) and the firm directory has a founder/managing-partner target, the composer now overrides the normal workflow-pain draft with a short gatekeeper-style referral email. The copy uses the Precise-connection opener from the cold-call script, names the founder/managing partner explicitly, asks for that person's best email address, and suppresses the usual audit-link footer to keep the note brief.
- **Why:** Non-leadership recipients are better treated like gatekeepers than buyers. A short "trying to reach {founder}" ask is more aligned with the call playbook and less likely to trigger a long irrelevant workflow pitch to someone who cannot buy.
