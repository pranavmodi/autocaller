# Decisions — 2026-08-24

## D-2026-08-24-01 — Preserve email thread ancestry across transports
- **Area:** deliverability
- **Status:** accepted
- **Decision:** Durable email actions store approved RFC `In-Reply-To` and `References` values and propagate them through Resend, SMTP, and Zoho Mail API. Provider record IDs are rejected as ancestry.
- **Why:** A `Re:` subject alone does not create a real email reply thread, and follow-ups may switch from an original Resend send to Zoho delivery.
- **Refs:** `app/services/email_notification_service.py`, `app/services/action_execution.py`, `app/cli.py`
