# Decisions - 2026-09-25 (UTC)

## D-2026-09-25-01 - Firm aliases require canonical or operator-verified identity
- **Area:** data-arch
- **Decision:** Possible OS resolves firm domains only from canonical websites or
  source-backed operator-verified alternate domains. Contact email domains,
  vendor/referral domains, shared service roots, and free-form research values
  remain evidence and cannot become identity aliases. The alias index is
  auditable and transactionally rebuildable from those trusted sources.
- **Why:** The previous EmailTag mirror promoted every contact email domain and
  every upstream observed domain into `firm_intel_aliases`, allowing unrelated
  records to hijack jobs and research. A production rebuild removed 925
  untrusted rows and reassigned 87 canonical domains while preserving legacy-ID
  and vanity aliases.
- **Refs:** `app/services/firm_alias_integrity.py`,
  `app/services/firm_intel_sync.py`, `app/services/pif_firm_crud.py`


## D-2026-09-25-02 — Separate durable browser applications from email sends
- **Area:** product
- **Status:** accepted
- **Decision:** Add an explicitly authorized Playwright/structured-LLM action loop, durable run/event records, question-and-answer controls, and CLI parity. Website confirmation stays separate from Zoho Sent verification. Persist potential submission before clicking; uncertainty locks further submission. ## D-2026-09-25-12 — Preserve application browsers across worker restarts

- **Area:** infra
- **Status:** accepted
- **Decision:** Move Playwright ownership and network guards into an independent systemd service with private Unix RPC. Workers reconnect; observation tokens and action IDs prevent stale or replayed actions. Preserve DB submission locks and require guarded restart after genuine browser loss.
