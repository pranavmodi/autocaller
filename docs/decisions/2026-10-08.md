## D-2026-10-08-01 — Measure job-source coverage and separate collection from judgment
- **Area:** data-arch
- **Status:** accepted
- **Decision:** Job searches paginate supported public APIs and learned employer ATS boards into one normalized listing shape, persist a per-source coverage record, use batched TypeSafe Jev probabilities for high-recall relevance ranking, and retain source-backed research for final verification.
- **Why:** Direct collection prevents an LLM from having to rediscover every listing, durable coverage makes omissions and failures visible, and Jev is faster and cheaper for bounded classification without weakening employer, role, or source verification.
- **Refs:** `app/services/job_search_source_adapters.py`, `app/services/job_search_relevance.py`, `j2061008a001`

## D-2026-10-08-02 — Retain candidate-level screening evidence per search run
- **Area:** data-arch
- **Status:** accepted
- **Decision:** Persist every normalized structured listing before screening and attach its Jev judgment, probability distribution, confidence, input hash, selection state or error. Enforce uniqueness within a run by canonical listing URL, retain separate snapshots across later runs, and keep canonical job-queue deduplication unchanged.
- **Why:** Operators need to audit what Jev accepted and rejected without losing historical comparisons or creating duplicate jobs.
- **Refs:** `app/services/job_search_screening.py`, `k2061008a002`
