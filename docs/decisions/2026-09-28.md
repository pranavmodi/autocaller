# Decisions — 2026-09-28

## D-2026-09-28-01 — Recover job-board failures onto exact official application pages
- **Area:** product
- **Status:** accepted
- **Decision:** A failed model-audited pre-form navigation triggers a bounded search for the exact role on the employer site or official ATS. The worker verifies employer, role and application evidence, then continues in a fresh browser on that official page.
- **Why:** Aggregator overlays and sign-in walls should not stall an authorized application or be confused with a possible submission, while exact-role verification prevents applying through a generic talent pool or different opening.
- **Refs:** `app/services/job_application_source.py`, `app/services/job_browser.py`
