# Decisions — 2026-09-24

## D-2026-09-24-01 — Collect reviews only with nightly firm maintenance
- **Area:** data-arch
- **Status:** accepted
- **Decision:** Review Alerts no longer schedules independent firm research. A law firm's review check follows its nightly profile-maintenance selection, and stored reviews with verified publication dates in the current 14-day window feed the existing alert composer.
- **Why:** The separate five-minute monitor, broad nightly review cohort and completed bulk corpus campaign duplicated work and created a large OpenClaw backlog without improving immediate alert coverage.
- **Refs:** `app/services/pif_research_maintenance.py`; `app/services/review_alerts.py`; `docs/REVIEW_ALERTS.md`

## D-2026-09-24-02 — Rank verified job sources instead of imposing a Jev cutoff
- **Area:** product
- **Status:** accepted; supersedes D-2026-09-23-04's fixed-threshold portion
- **Decision:** Score every freshly fetched eligible role source with TypeSafe Jev, persist all probabilities, and use the highest-probability source. Do not reject an otherwise verified role because its best Jev probability falls below a fixed cutoff. Keep official-domain ownership, live-page fetching, semantic role identity and exact evidence validation as deterministic gates; do not require the source host to be on a fixed allowlist.
- **Why:** ENTER's official site and Lever board established the Business Process Engineer role, but a single selected page scored 77% against an 80% cutoff. The cutoff discarded corroborated evidence instead of choosing the strongest available source.
- **Refs:** `app/services/job_agent_research.py`; `docs/JOB_AGENT.md`; supersedes threshold policy in D-2026-09-23-04

## D-2026-09-24-03 — Classify contract status once at job extraction
- **Area:** product
- **Status:** accepted
- **Decision:** Use a batched TypeSafe Jev Choice judgment at job extraction to store `contract`, `non_contract`, or `unknown` with probabilities, confidence, model, timestamp and input hash. Application preparation and UI filters only read the saved judgment. Backfill existing Job Agent rows once and mirror the result to matching Leads job listings.
- **Why:** Contract status is a property of the source job, so recomputing it during each application adds latency and can produce inconsistent answers for the same listing.
- **Refs:** `app/services/job_contract_classification.py`; `app/services/daily_career_search.py`; `app/services/pif_job_posting_research.py`; `docs/JOB_AGENT.md`

## D-2026-09-24-04 — Do not gate corroborating role sources by hostname
- **Area:** product
- **Status:** accepted
- **Decision:** A corroborating role page may come from any freshly fetched source. The verifier no longer rejects it solely because its hostname is outside the employer domain or a hardcoded job-board allowlist. Employer identity, role evidence, semantic Jev identity and exact quoted evidence remain required.
- **Why:** Smaller recruiting partners and regional aggregators can publish legitimate openings that are absent from a fixed platform list. Host allowlisting was a brittle proxy for evidence quality and blocked otherwise verifiable applications such as the ENTER role.
- **Refs:** `app/services/job_agent_research.py`; `docs/JOB_AGENT.md`
