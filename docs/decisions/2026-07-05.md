## D-2026-07-05-02 — Sync client hardened: 25-item pages + 5xx retry, not server-side fixes
- **Area:** data-arch
- **Decision:** The v2 delta feed builds full profiles per item (~11s at
  limit=100) and 502s under load; the client now defaults to
  `FIRM_INTEL_PAGE_SIZE=25` and retries 5xx/transport errors 4x with backoff.
  Watermark advances only after a fully successful run, so failed crawls
  re-cover safely.
- **Why:** Client-side resilience ships now; server-side profile-build
  performance is EmailTag work, deferred until the 502 rate under normal load
  proves it necessary.
- **Refs:** app/services/firm_intel_sync.py (_page_size, _get_with_retry)

## D-2026-07-05-04 — Full crawls are resumable; restart-from-zero was starving the tail
- **Area:** data-arch
- **Decision:** `sync_firm_intel` full crawls persist a resume point
  (`resume_cursor` + `resume_watermark` in `firm_intel_sync_state.last_result`)
  on failure and continue from it on the next full run; `pif sync --restart`
  discards it. Watermark still only advances when the crawl chain reaches the
  end of the feed. Limited/delta runs neither consume nor wipe resume points.
- **Why:** The emailtag v2 feed tolerates a bounded number of requests per
  session before 502ing (worker memory pressure, confirmed by its operator).
  Restarting every attempt from page 0 re-spent that budget on already-synced
  pages — after the budget shrank below ~150 pages the tail became permanently
  unreachable. Resume makes every attempt monotonic forward progress.
- **Refs:** app/services/firm_intel_sync.py, tests/test_firm_intel_sync.py,
  docs/cli.md (§3 pif sync row)
