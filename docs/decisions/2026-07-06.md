# Decisions — 2026-07-06 (UTC)

## D-2026-07-06-01 — LexVisibility report philosophy: demonstrate expertise, make complexity legible, sell the capability
- **Area:** product
- **Decision:** The report moves from diagnosis-only to a McKinsey-style
  document where every section leads with a "so what" line and an executive
  implication. New structure: executive summary → evidence → mechanism (with
  named, cited evidence per rival — expertise proof) → why-this-is-hard
  (multi-surface work + answer drift ⇒ a standing capability, not a one-time
  fix) → the prize (upside model, not just loss) → three free evidence-backed
  first moves → the campaign at workstream level (shape, not operating
  manual) → CTA "walk through the full plan". Refines HANDOFF settled
  decision #5 (diagnosis-only): the fix's *shape* is now shown because
  complexity made legible is the buying argument; the operating detail
  remains the meeting.
- **Why:** The founder should conclude on his own that (a) these people
  obviously know what they're talking about (specificity, not adjectives),
  (b) the problem is too complex for DIY or his SEO agency, and (c) the
  upside is big enough to pay for. Discipline rule: every claim in the
  expertise/upside sections must trace to a scan artifact — generic
  consultant prose reads as a funnel and kills trust.
