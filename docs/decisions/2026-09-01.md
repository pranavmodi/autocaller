# Decisions — 2026-09-01

## D-2026-09-01-01 — Preserve review evidence across collection reruns
- **Area:** data-arch
- **Status:** accepted
- **Decision:** Public review research is incremental and idempotent: fresh source responses merge into stored evidence, while corpus progress distinguishes raw, distinct, independent, and classified records.
- **Why:** Bounded public source payloads can shrink between requests; replacing stored data would erase valid evidence and make the 5,000-review gate unreliable.
- **Refs:** app/services/firm_review_research.py

## D-2026-09-01-02 — Make review collection additive and corpus-gated
- **Area:** data-arch
- **Status:** active
- **Decision:** Merge source-backed reviews idempotently, preserve prior evidence, attach stable hashes and collection timestamps, prioritize canonical PI firms, and expose the 5,000-review gate through CLI, API, and UI.
- **Why:** The previous worker replaced reviews_json with each bounded source response and offered no corpus-level progress or bulk queue controls.
- **Refs:** app/services/firm_review_research.py; app/api/firm_reviews.py; app/cli.py

- **Area:** data-arch
- **Status:** accepted
- **Decision:** Freeze the publication corpus at `2026-09-01T07:28:42Z`, after the collection queue drained, and report independent-source and firm-balanced findings separately from curated and aggregator records.
